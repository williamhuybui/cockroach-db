# Role and Responsibility

## Team

### Huy — Captain / Fullstack Data Scientist
- Full-stack development: front-end, back-end, and deployment
- Helps the team break ties if we get stuck on a decision
- Assigns and rebalances tasks across the team

### Hieu — Solution Architect / Data Admin
- Sets up cloud infrastructure and database needed for the build
- Owns the solution architecture from an infra standpoint
- Maintains and updates the submission / showcase page

### Ha — R&D Lead
- Researches techniques, libraries, and approaches as needed
- Makes judgment calls on architecture and research-heavy decisions
- Pairs with Huy on ramp-up as needed

### Tri — Project Manager / Coordinator
- Proactively schedules team meetings and check-ins
- Runs through the meeting agenda / bullet points each time
- Tracks progress and keeps everyone on schedule
- Open role otherwise, jumping in wherever needed

## Focus Areas

**Driver** — the person in charge of that piece of work: owns it, drives decisions, does the hands-on execution.

**Passenger** — supports under that lead: assists, reviews, and follows the driver's direction rather than owning the outcome.
