# Roofing Business Interview Script

Framed based on Mr.Huy guidance so the conversation would stays on scope.

---

## Introductions and Framing (~5-10 min)

1. What our project is about (An AI phone agent with persistent memory, for service businesses that can't staff their phones 24/7.)
2. What we're trying to build (automatic answering, conversation storage, admin dashboard)
3. What is his current business problems ( have a deeper understanding of the business overall )
4. What we need from him today (how his calls currently work, what a good call looks like, what he'd want to see on a dashboard)
5. What problem of his our solution could help with — *Frame it based on his previous answers and try to hear more about his story to note it down*


---

## Core Questions (~15-20 min) 

**Current call handling**
1. On an average day, how many calls come in, and how many are missed or after-hours?
2. Walk me through a normal call — what do you or your staff ask, and where does that info go afterward?
3. Who's responsible for calling a customer back, and how do you know it's been done?

**What the AI needs to capture**
4. If the AI could only ask five questions before ending the call, what should they be? 
5. What makes a lead urgent vs. one that can wait?
6. Are there calls you'd never want the AI handling on its own — where it should hand off to a person?

**Memory / repeat callers**
7. When the same customer calls again, what do you want to already know about them (address, prior issue, appointment, etc.)?
8. What should happen automatically after a call ends — a task, a text, an alert?

**Dashboard / success**
9. What are the 2-3 things you'd want to see first thing each morning?
10. After 30 days of using something like this, what result would make you want to keep paying for it?

---

## Backup Questions (only if time allows / conversation naturally goes there)

- Emergency definition and after-hours handling
- Appointment scheduling logic (service area, rotation, daily limits)
- Multiple properties / multiple callers per property
- Confirmation and reminder preferences (call/text/email)
- Job types the business doesn't accept

---

## Closing — Confirm Scope for Us

1. Of everything we discussed, which 2-3 features would matter most to you first?
2. Is there anything the AI should absolutely never do without a human checking it first?
