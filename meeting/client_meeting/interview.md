# Roofing Business Interview 

## 1. Understand the Business and Customers

1. Does the business mainly handle roof repair, replacement, inspection, or emergencies?
2. Are most customers homeowners, property managers, or commercial clients?
3. Which type of job is the most valuable or important to the business?
4. Who currently answers calls and manages leads?

---

## 2. Current Call-Handling Process 

1. How many calls does the business receive on an average day?
2. How many calls are missed or happen outside business hours?
3. What questions do staff usually ask when a customer calls?
4. Where is the information entered after the call?
5. Who is responsible for calling the customer back?
6. How do you know whether a customer has already been called back?
7. Can you describe a recent normal call that staff handles?

---

## 3. Call Types and Information the AI Should Collect

1. What are the most common types of calls?
   - New estimate
   - Roof leak
   - Storm or hail damage
   - Existing customer
   - Warranty request
   - Reschedule
   - Billing
   - Spam or vendor
2. For a new customer, what information must be collected before the call ends?
3. If the AI could ask only five questions, what would the five most important questions be?
4. Does the business need to collect:
   - Name and phone number?
   - Property address?
   - Whether the caller is the homeowner?
   - Type of roofing problem?
   - Roof type or roof age?
   - Insurance claim status?
   - Desired timeline?
5. What makes a lead urgent or high priority?
6. Which types of jobs does the business not accept?
7. When should the AI immediately transfer the call to a person?

---

## 4. Emergency, Appointment, and Site Visit 

1. How does the business define a roofing emergency?
2. How are active leaks, fallen trees, or ceiling damage handled?
3. Is anyone available after business hours?
4. How long does each visit usually take?
5. How are appointments assigned?
   - Service area?
   - Availability?
   - Rotation?
   - Type of job?
6. Is there a daily appointment limit?
7. Should customers receive confirmation and reminders by phone, text, or email?

---

## 5. Customer Memory, Callback, and Tasks 

1. When a customer calls again, what information should staff see first?
2. What should the AI remember?
   - Property address
   - Previous issue
   - Appointment
   - Uploaded photos
   - Previous estimate
   - Assigned salesperson
   - Open callback task
3. Can one customer have multiple properties?
4. Can multiple people call about the same property?
5. What information should the AI verify before discussing customer history?
6. What tasks should the system create after a call?
7. What are the most common task types?
   - Call the customer back
   - Schedule an inspection
   - Review photos
   - Send an estimate
   - Contact insurance
   - Handle a warranty request
8. Who should be assigned to each task type?
9. How quickly should customers be called back?
10. How do you know whether a task has been accepted and completed?
11. Should the business receive call summaries by text, email, or dashboard?

---

## 6. Dashboard and Success Metrics 

1. What three pieces of information should the owner see first about call each morning?
2. Which problems require immediate alerts?
3. Who will use the dashboard: the owner, office manager, or sales team?
4. After 30 days, what result would make the business continue paying for it?

---

## 7. Confirm the MVP 

1. If the team could build only three features first, which three would you choose?
2. Which features are must-haves?
3. Which features can be added later?
4. Which activities should the AI not perform/ or perform under human review?
5. Which AI mistake would be unacceptable?
