# ~28-second demo script

Covers two sections of the landing page: the hero ("The phone rings. Something
always picks up.") and the impact section ("Why speed-to-lead matters").

Written in plain, simple language so it's easy to read out loud.

---

## Part 1 — Hero (0:00–0:12)

> The phone is everything for a small business. Whoever answers first gets
> the job. Most businesses can't answer it all day and night. Openline AI
> can — it answers every call and remembers every caller.

## Part 2 — Impact / roofing example (0:12–0:28)

> Take roofing. Sixty-two percent of calls go unanswered. Eighty-five percent
> of those callers never call back — they call the next roofer. That's up to
> one hundred twenty thousand dollars a year, lost. Openline AI covers the
> phone, 24/7.

---

## Notes

- ~75 words total — about 28 seconds at a normal, confident speaking pace.
- Numbers are spelled out ("sixty-two percent," "one hundred twenty thousand
  dollars") so they're easy to say out loud. The on-screen stat cards can
  still show the actual numerals (62%, 85%, $120K).
- Pause briefly after "Take roofing." — that's the pivot from the general
  pitch to the specific example.
